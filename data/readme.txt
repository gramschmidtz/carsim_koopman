Format Description:
1: sampling time
2-4: global X(unit: m), Y(unit: m), Yaw angle(unit: deg)
5-7: local Vx(unit: km/h), Vy(unit: km/h), ayw rate(unit: deg/s)
8-9: global Vx(unit: km/h), Vy(unit: km/h)
10-12: accelerations of the CoG: ax(unit: m/s^2), ay(unit: m/s^2), a_yaw(unit: rad/s^2)
13: slip angle(unit: deg)
14-15: Left and right front angle(unit: deg)
16: The longitudinal of the front axle(unit: N)
17-18: Fx_L1 and Fx_R1
19: Fy_A1 --> The lateral of the front axle(unit: N)
20-21: Fy_L1 and Fy_R1
22: Fx_A2
23-24: Fx_L2 and Fx_R2
25: Fy_A2
26-27: Fy_L2 and Fy_R2
28-30: the steering wheel angle(deg), throttle, brake
